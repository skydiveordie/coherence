About us

Surfaces composed like music

Coherence Group designs and supplies quartz slabs for kitchens, bathrooms, commercial spaces and building façades. Every surface is high-density engineered quartz — non-porous, durable and made to last for decades of everyday life.

Every decor in our collection bears the name of an opera. This is more than poetry: each slab has its own character, its own tempo and its own stage where it sounds best — from an intimate aria to a grand, dramatic composition.

Why quartz

Benefits you feel every day

Non-porous and hygienic

The compact surface does not absorb liquids or stains, does not harbour bacteria and needs no sealing.

Wear resistance

Exceptional hardness and resistance to scratches, impact and heat — for surfaces in everyday use.

Colour stability

Tone and pattern stay true for years — no fading in light and no change with use.

Easy maintenance

Water and a mild detergent are all it takes. No special products, no polishing, no worries.
